# Image Prompt — Top Panel (1:3)

Generate the **top half** of a 1:6 vertical Roguelike Your Life report as a standalone 1:3 poster panel.

## Fixed text

Main title: `ROGUELIKE YOUR LIFE`

Subtitle: `TURN YOUR REALITY INTO A ROGUELIKE RUN`

Secondary line: `CURRENT SAVE FILE · LIFE RUN ANALYSIS`

## Style

Use an original mythic-underworld action-roguelike visual language inspired by the energy of Hades: heroic character portrait, black-stone UI, warm-gold borders, crimson and orange flame, limited teal spectral light, laurel motifs, Greek geometry, altar and temple details. Keep it sharp, elegant, cinematic, and highly readable. Do not make gothic horror the dominant style.

## Sections

Generate only:

1. Character Overview
2. Core Stats
3. Passive Traits
4. Equipped Relics / Tools
5. Debuffs / Curses

## Rules

- A mobile viewport should show only about 1–1.5 major sections.
- The protagonist must dominate the cover region.
- Relic cards should feel collectible and game-native.
- Debuffs may use red-black hazard styling without turning the entire report into horror.
- End with a strong continuation divider: `THE RUN CONTINUES…`
- Do not include section 6.
- Use concise English labels. Avoid tiny paragraphs inside the image.
